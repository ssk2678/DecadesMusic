DecadesMusic is the result of being in tune with the music scene across four decades, essentially half the span of one's living years.

Each decade offers something special - either a new unique sound, a new genre, or a masterful blending of such from prior decades.

Though the compilation of songs here represent a trifling of all the music produced over the last four decades, we made our best effort to offer a generous sampling - one that captures the mood and ambiance of each decade and the transition between them.

It is our hope that the songs and videos in each selection invoke special thoughts, feelings, and memories.
